Was it because I’m an agent?

The sheer terror made me let out a sigh.

“Sir.”

“I’m not doing this because I’m an agent. ...It’s just simple logic.”

I looked at the rescued person, who was still in the corner, trembling as he fumbled with his flayed skin.

If we can all survive together, there’s no need to kill anyone, is there?

“.......”

“Do you think it would be better for you to be in a safe place?”

“Hik—hng....”

Then he glanced fearfully at Baek Saheon. Baek Saheon regarded him with a face gone cold, as though he were a variable that might threaten his survival....

But I didn’t react to either of them. Instead, I spoke slowly.

“Alright. Then we’ll move to a safe place.”

I supported the rescued person and went up one more floor via the emergency stairs.
